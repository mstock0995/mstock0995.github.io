PhD Student, Management Science & Operations Management
University of Chicago Booth School of Business

I am a fifth-year PhD student in Management Science and Operations Management at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business, advised by Levi DeValve.

I study how firms can make effective operational decisions in complex networks under uncertainty. Two questions guide my work: how much data do we need to make reliable decisions, and how can we design practical algorithms that are guaranteed to perform well? I use the structure of operational problems to answer these questions.

Much of my research focuses on inventory and fulfillment networks, where firms must order and place inventory before demand is known, then decide how to fulfill orders as they arrive. My research interests include supply chain management, stochastic and data-driven optimization, and online resource allocation.
